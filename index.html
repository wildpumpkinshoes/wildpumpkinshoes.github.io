Cyberpsychology research

What happens when the thing on your screen starts to feel like someone? That question drives most of my work. I'm especially curious about AI companions, young people, and what helps us stay grounded.

Thesis

Nature and AI companion dependency

Can time outdoors help people who lean hard on AI companion apps? My master's thesis looks at nature-based interventions as a way to loosen that grip.

Paper

Parasocial reinforcement in adolescents

When chatbots act human, teens can bond with them in ways that matter. This paper explores how that anthropomorphism may connect to internalizing disorders like anxiety and depression.

Youth mentorship

Growing up is hard enough without a manual. I work with transition-age young adults on the big stuff: jobs, school, housing, and figuring out what independence actually looks like for them.

Goals first
